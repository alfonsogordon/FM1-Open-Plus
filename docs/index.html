FM1 Open Plus Sound Lab v0.1

Playable browser prototype. Original Web Audio sound models — not Skiff firmware or an FM1 hardware emulator. No hardware flashing.

Tap Enable audio, then play the keys.

Six-track idea: generative pattern sketch

Three melodic tracks and three synthesised drum lanes. Each square toggles a step. Pitch and rhythm mutations are independent. This is a sketch of the intended workflow, not a port of Jarreth.

Keyboard: A S D F G H J K. Audio starts only after a user gesture. For best timing, keep the browser tab foregrounded. MIDI clock and firmware integration are not implemented.
